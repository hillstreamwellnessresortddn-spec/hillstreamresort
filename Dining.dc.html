The table

Everyone eats together at eight, at one table that seats twenty-two. Guests who want to eat alone can ask for a tray in the room, and about one in ten does on the first night and then never again.

Breakfast is looser: served at the stream pavilion from seven until ten, ordered off a short card. Lunch is a single dish, left out at one for whoever is around.

The kitchen grows what it can on the four terraces below it. Everything else comes up the hill twice a week.
